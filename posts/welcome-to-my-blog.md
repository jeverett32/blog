I've never considered myself a writer. In fact, I hate writing. Like with a passion. I even chose a major where I would basically never need to take a writing class or be graded on my writing. One of the biggest reasons I love AI is because of how good it is at turning my ideas into coherent sentences.

Now it's Thanksgiving break. I didn't have class all week, so I went home to my parents' to chill and waste away until classes start again. I've been working through a course so I can take the GitHub Foundations Certification, and I've also been working on a few personal projects. Then, out of nowhere, it came into my head that I should start a blog.

_Why in the world would that come into my head? I have no clue. It's a terrible idea actually. Why would I spend my time doing something I hate when there are sooo many other important things I need to be working on?_

## Why I'm Doing This

Well, I reasoned with myself. Writing actually is a super important skill. In my career, I am going to be communicating with people constantly. I am in a technical field but want to lean into a managerial position in the future. It's important that I can communicate, persuade, and explain hard concepts. In the end, I decided that by writing blogs and tutorials, I could help my future self gain some much-needed skills.

Also, I think having a blog is pretty cool.

(It also makes me feel like a 50-year-old mom. Who even writes blogs anymore?)

## What to Expect

Basically, this blog will have several different types of articles:

* **Tutorials:** When I learn new skills, I will turn them into step-by-step tutorials here. This will help me solidify those skills and document them for future use.

* **Opinions:** There are so many changes happening in the tech industry. AI is evolving quickly and new tools are coming out every day. Having an outlet to express my opinion on new technology will motivate me to go out and try new tools.

* **Life Updates:** This is a place where I can share updates on important events and projects such as internships, competitions, or personal projects.

---

So, these blogs really aren't for anyone to see. They are more for my own benefit, but if someone comes across it, that's even better. Hopefully, they can get an idea of how my brain works, or even find some helpful technical tips.
